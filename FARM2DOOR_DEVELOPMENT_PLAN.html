1. Executive Summary & Problem Analysis

Farm2Door is a specialized, three-tier agricultural e-commerce platform designed to link smallholder farmers directly with end consumers, food service businesses, and urban retailers. By providing a centralized digital storefront, direct farm-gate inventory listings, integrated payment verification via Paystack, and verified peer reviews, the platform bypasses predatory middlemen networks, minimizes post-harvest produce spoilage, and enforces price transparency across the agrarian economy of Ekiti State.

Core Problem Statement Addressed
  • Intermediary Monopoly: 3 to 5 layers of aggregators, wholesalers, and retail brokers extract up to 50–70% of the final retail price, leaving smallholder farmers with suppressed farm-gate returns.
  • Post-Harvest Produce Loss: Delayed physical negotiations and transit hurdles cause an estimated 40% loss of fresh produce (tomatoes, peppers, leafy vegetables, tubers) before consumption.
  • Information Asymmetry: Farmers lack visibility into prevailing consumer demand and competitive market pricing across urban consuming clusters.
  • Lack of Agricultural-Specific Commerce Systems: Mainstream marketplaces cater to non-perishable goods and lack support for harvest packaging units (tubers, baskets, crates, bundles, 50kg bags) or rapid inventory turnover.

4. Database Architecture & Schema Summary

The persistence tier runs on PostgreSQL 15+ hosted on Supabase, structured in Third Normal Form (3NF) with specialized composite indexing for agrarian query patterns.

Table Name Primary Key Key Foreign Relationships Purpose & Constraints
users id (UUID) Linked to auth.users Stores profiles, roles ('farmer', 'buyer', 'admin'), farm community, and NUBAN settlement bank details.
categories id (BIGSERIAL) None Stores the 6 PRD produce categories with slug and descriptive metadata.
products id (BIGSERIAL) farmer_id → users.id, category_id → categories.id Harvest catalog with packaging unit check constraint, stock quantity, and price.
orders id (BIGSERIAL) buyer_id → users.id Order header with unique reference (FD-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX) and lifecycle status.
order_items id (BIGSERIAL) order_id → orders.id, product_id → products.id Line items recording unit price snapshot and quantity purchased.
payments id (BIGSERIAL) order_id → orders.id Financial audit ledger with Paystack reference, channel, amount, and timestamp.
reviews id (BIGSERIAL) product_id → products.id, buyer_id → users.id Post-delivery ratings (1-5 integer) and qualitative customer feedback.
